About BeanArts

More Than a Vendor —
Your Brand's Creative Partner

Founded in 2018, BeanArts began with a simple but powerful belief: corporate gifting deserves to be as thoughtful and premium as the brands that give them. We set out to replace generic catalogue gifts with intentional, beautifully crafted brand experiences.

Today we're India's premier corporate gifting and custom printing partner — trusted by 50+ brands from agile startups to global enterprises. Our Bengaluru studio handles everything from concept design and material sourcing to production, quality control, and pan-India delivery.

Whether it's 50 welcome kits for a startup's first cohort or 10,000 Diwali hampers for a multinational — we bring the same meticulous attention to detail to every single order.
